Cross-Shareholdings

Policy shareholdings (政策保有株式) exactly as disclosed in each company's annual securities report: what each company holds, who holds whom back, and how fast the unwind is running. Coverage: every listed filer that discloses named policy holdings, five fiscal years of filings, each company shown on its latest.

Positions Reduced Against Positions Added

Show calculation A position counts as cut (or added) when its as-filed share count is lower (higher) than the prior-year column of the same filing. Two disclosed caveats: share-count additions can include stock splits (a 2×/3×/5× jump in shares with an unchanged position is usually a split, not a purchase); and the table's Δ value column compares fair-valued book values, so market price moves are inside it — a filer can be cutting positions while its Δ value rises in a rising market. Both input columns are official figures as filed; the comparisons are calculated by the platform.

Disclosed Holdings

How to read this table

Named policy holdings from the governance section (株式の保有状況) of the filing named above. Official statistics as filed. Missing values are shown as —, never as zero. Names are the filed ones in English and Japanese, written evenly on screen; the CSV keeps them exactly as filed.

Notes to this table, as filed

The filing's own numbered footnotes to the table above, verbatim. They are where a filer explains a share count that moved without a trade — a split, a consolidation, a merger. The disclosure numbers them (注)1, (注)2 … and gives no link back to a row, so a note is tied to a holding above only where it names that issuer.

    Holdings Whose Purpose Changed

    How to read this table

    Holdings this filer moved between a policy shareholding and 純投資目的 (pure investment), from the annual report's own 保有目的を変更した投資株式 table. Official statistics as filed. A holding moved to pure investment leaves the table above without necessarily being sold.

    Disclosed Holders

    How to read this table

    Every filer whose own annual report names this company among its policy holdings. This view exists only because every filer's table is in one database — no single filing shows it.

    Source: 有価証券報告書 (annual securities reports) via EDINET, Financial Services Agency. Every figure links to its filing; every raw file is archived with its SHA-256. Company names are the filed names in both languages, written evenly on screen (capitals and letter widths tidied; every CSV keeps them exactly as filed): a filing names its holdings in Japanese, and each company's own annual report states its English name on the cover page. Where a company files no annual report, EDINET's filer registry is used instead; where neither states one, the Japanese name is shown alone, never blank. Sector coverage and extraction status are stated above — gaps are disclosed, never filled.
