Are Japan's real wages rising?

Definition

Real wages are what workers earn after allowing for the rise in prices. The Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare's Monthly Labour Survey (毎月勤労統計調査) measures total cash earnings per worker, including overtime and bonuses, and deflates them by the consumer price index excluding imputed rent.

The change on a year earlier on this page is the Ministry's own published rate, not one calculated here. It covers all industries and establishments with five or more employees. The figures are the final ones; the Ministry's preliminary figures come out a few weeks earlier and are not used.

The published rate is used rather than one calculated from the wage index because the index steps up between December 2023 and January 2024, so a change calculated across that point overstates growth.
