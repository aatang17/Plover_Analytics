Privacy Policy

Plover Analytics is run by an individual, Aaron Jian Ye Tang. This page says what we collect, why, who receives it, how long it is kept and how to have it deleted. Last updated 7 November 2026. Questions go to info@ploveranalytics.com.

What We Collect

Visits
Each visit is counted under a one-way code made from your internet address, your browser's description and a secret that changes daily. We keep the code, the pages you read, the page that sent you, how long a page was visible, your country and the name of your network (your internet provider, or your employer's network at work). If you are signed in, we also keep the domain of your email address (the part after the @). The Methodology page sets out the details.
Cookies
Only if you accept: one cookie with a random number, kept for a year, to tell a returning reader from a new one, and, if you accept ad measurement, Reddit's ad click identifier for up to 30 days. Declining stores only your choice. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control, we set no cookie.
Server log
Our web server's technical log records the internet address of each request. It is overwritten as it fills.
Your account
Your email address, when you joined and last signed in, and the tagged link you arrived by, if any. When you ask for a sign-in link we keep the email, the internet address the request came from and the time.
What you keep in it
Your chats, watchlist, lists, studies and saved notes the assistant remembers about you. API keys you add are stored encrypted, and only their last four characters are ever shown. A Google Drive connection is read-only. A Telegram link stores your Telegram username and chat.
Questions
A question typed into a question box is kept: its text (up to 300 characters), the time and the page it was asked on, with no code, address or account attached. Questions asked in the Plover Assistant panel are kept with the day's visitor code, the country and the network name. Questions asked through Telegram are kept with a one-way reader code.
AI assistants such as ChatGPT
When an assistant uses Plover for you, it sends us only the request, such as a company code or a dataset. We answer it and do not store it. The visit count records that a request arrived, with the assistant company's network name and country. The assistant's own privacy policy covers your conversation with it.

Why We Use It

To answer your questions, keep your account and what you save in it, send sign-in emails, stop abuse, count readers, and check and improve answers: typed questions are reviewed and asked again to test that answers are still right. We do not sell personal data, show advertising or load third-party analytics scripts.

Who Receives It

AI providers
To write an answer, the question and your previous question are sent to DeepSeek or OpenAI. Questions about news go to OpenAI's web search. A desk that uses your own key sends your request to the provider you chose: Anthropic, OpenAI or DeepSeek.
Resend
Sends sign-in emails, so it receives your email address and the link.
DigitalOcean and Cloudflare
DigitalOcean hosts the servers. Cloudflare's network sits in front of the site and handles every request.
Backup storage
A copy of the account database is stored each night with a cloud storage service.
Telegram
Our own alert chat receives the text of typed questions, with nothing about who asked.
Reddit
Only if you accept ad measurement: when you create an account, Reddit receives the click identifier, the sign-up time and a random event code, never your email or address.

How Long It Is Kept

Sign-in links
Work for 30 minutes. A session lasts 30 days and is renewed while you use it.
Account, saved items, questions and visit records
Kept until you ask us to delete them. Nothing deletes them automatically yet.
Backups
Daily copies are kept for 14 days and monthly copies for 12 months, so deleted data can remain in a backup for up to 12 months.

Your Choices

Cookies
"Cookie settings" at the foot of any page changes your choice.
In your account
You can delete memory notes, lists, API keys, data sources, studies and the Google Drive connection yourself. Signing out ends the session. Sending /disconnect to the Telegram bot removes the link.
A copy or deletion
Email info@ploveranalytics.com from the address you sign in with to get a copy of your data or to delete your account and everything in it. We act within 30 days.

Children

Plover Analytics is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
