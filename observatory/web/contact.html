Academics

The data and the API are open, with no key or account. For a result others can reproduce, cite the API address with as_of=YYYY-MM-DD: it returns the data as it stood on that date. Each source's own citation is under Attribution on the Methodology page.

Write to us about a dataset your research needs, bulk downloads, or an error you find.

Journalists

Charts on the data pages download as an image with the source printed on it, and as a CSV. Link to the page you used so readers can check the figure. The Methodology page sets out how every rate is calculated.

Write to us to confirm a figure or how it was worked out. Include your publication and deadline.

Investors

A free account keeps every answer in full, your questions as chats, a watchlist of what changed and what is coming up, and charts and screens that update themselves. Sign in with your email; there is no password.

Write to us about a company or market we do not cover, a figure you rely on, or using the data in your own tools through the API or an AI assistant.

Data Teams

Every figure is kept as each release published it, and a revision is stored as a new release. A backtest can therefore use only what was known on a given date.

Write to us about the datasets you need, how often you pull them, and how you want them delivered.
