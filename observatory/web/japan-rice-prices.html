How have rice prices in Japan changed over the past year?

Definition

The rice index is the rice group of Japan's national Consumer Price Index, compiled by the Statistics Bureau of Japan from the prices households pay in shops. It is published monthly, in the second half of the following month, on a 2025 = 100 base.

The rate quoted here compares the rice index with the same month a year earlier. Headline CPI, all items, is shown beside it for comparison.

Index levels are published to one decimal place. Rates on this page are calculated from those published levels, so a rate can differ from the Bureau's own published rate by up to 0.1 percentage points; the method is stated under Show calculation.
