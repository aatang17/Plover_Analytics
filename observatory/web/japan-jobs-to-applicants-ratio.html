What is Japan's jobs-to-applicants ratio?

Definition

The jobs-to-applicants ratio (有効求人倍率) is the number of job openings registered at Hello Work, Japan's public employment offices, for every person registered there looking for a job. The Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare publishes it every month, on the same day as the unemployment rate.

A ratio above 1 means there are more openings than applicants. It counts only jobs and job seekers registered at public offices, so it measures how tight that part of the labour market is, not the whole of it.

The figure here includes part-time jobs and is seasonally adjusted, exactly as published to two decimal places. The ratio of new openings to new applications in the month is shown beside it.
