What is Japan's unemployment rate?

Definition

The unemployment rate is the number of people out of work and looking for it, as a share of the labour force: everyone aged 15 and over who is working or looking for work. The Statistics Bureau of Japan publishes it every month from its Labour Force Survey, a survey of households, at the end of the following month.

The rate on this page is the seasonally adjusted figure, exactly as published to one decimal place. It is not recalculated here. Changes are stated in percentage points, the difference between two rates.

The labour force, employment and unemployment counts behind it, by sex and by age, are on the labour page.
