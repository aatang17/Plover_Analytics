        Your Reading

        Save any article you are reading to Plover, with a summary. Pages you can read behind a paywall are saved too, as text, and only you can see them.

        Chrome extension

        Download the Extension

        1. Unzip the download.
        2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode.
        3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder. Then click the Plover button on any article.

        Any browser: a bookmark

        Save to Plover

        Drag this button to your bookmarks bar once. Then click the bookmark on an article.
