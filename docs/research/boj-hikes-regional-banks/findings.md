# Findings — BoJ rate increases and regional banks

All numbers from `data/numbers.json` (written by `stats.py` from the files logged in `log.md`).

## §2 Rate path and pass-through
Call rate (monthly mean) −0.006% Feb 2024 → 0.978% Jul 2026 (+0.98 pp) in five steps. Regional-bank new-loan rate 0.691% → 1.752% (+1.06 pp); second-tier 0.865% → 1.798%. Posted ordinary deposit rate 0.001% → 0.255% Jul 2026 (+0.25 pp), 0.322% Sep 2026. Loan rates moved with the call rate; posted deposit rates about a quarter as much.

## §3 Sector result (JBA aggregates, ¥bn)
First-tier NII 3,066.7 (FY23) → 3,402.1 → 4,030.7 (+964.0, +31.4%); bond result −374.6, −368.2, −941.6. Second-tier NII 733.3 → 757.8 → 825.3 (+92.0, +12.5%); bond −94.0, −103.9, −194.5. Two-year extra NII vs FY23 level: 1,299.4 + 116.5 = 1,415.8; two-year realised bond result −1,309.9 − 298.4 = −1,608.3; net −192.4.
Source of the NII gain (94 unmerged banks, FY23→FY25): loan interest +1,142.6, securities +542.3, deposits with banks +245.3 (23% of the NII gain of 1,059.2); deposit interest +803.5 (×5.2), equal to 70% of the rise in loan interest.

## §4 Gainers — H3 supported, H2 disproven (reversed)
Median NII change +20.4% (94 banks); first-tier 28.0% (60), second-tier 9.4% (34). Range −5.2% (Fukuoka Chuo) to +62.0% (Ashikaga); seven banks no increase or a decline (six second-tier plus Hokuto). LDR vs gain: ρ = −0.26 (n=94); above-median-LDR banks median 16.2%, below 24.2%. Deposits-with-banks share of assets (Mar 2024, median 11.5%) vs gain: ρ = 0.57 — the strongest relationship found.

## §5 Cost — H1 disproven
40 of 92 banks net positive (two-year extra NII > two-year realised bond losses); 52 net negative. On the plan's simpler definition (FY25 vs FY23 NII change against two-year bond losses) 30 of 92. Medians: gain 4.5% of net assets, bond result −5.1%. Worst: Towa −37.7% (bond −¥38.4bn vs gain ¥6.0bn), Tochigi −24.7%, Hokuto −23.6%, Hokkoku −20.1% (bond −¥64.0bn vs gain ¥21.7bn, despite a +52.8% NII rise), Momiji −14.9%. Best: Nanto +9.6%, Ryukyus +7.3%, Gunma +7.2%, Shimane +7.0%, Iyo +6.5% (bond gains +3.0%). Unrealised AFS bond losses, 67 clean regional filers: −¥3,415.1bn (JGBs −¥1,911.8bn), median −13.9% of equity (filer level).

## §6 The next increase — H4 supported
84 banks with a March 2026 IRRBB1 including the parallel-up row. Largest ΔEVE scenario: parallel down 45, flattener 1 (falling-rate, 46); parallel up 26, steepener 12 (38). Tochigi 41.0% of Tier 1 from parallel down; its parallel-up ΔEVE 0.4%. Parallel-up ΔEVE median 4.4% of Tier 1; highest Kochi 13.9%, Higashi-Nippon 13.7%, Yamanashi Chuo 13.1%, Oita 12.6%, Fukuoka Chuo 12.3%; none above 15%. H4: top-ten overlap between realised bond cost and parallel-up ΔEVE is 2 (Oita, Chikuho), ρ = 0.01 (n=78).
ΔNII under parallel up (FSA convention: positive = fall): 81 banks report it; 57 show a fall, 17 a rise, 7 zero; median a fall of 0.45% of Tier 1. Largest fall Hokuto 16.6% of Tier 1; Ashikaga shows a fall of 6.9% despite the largest realised NII gain. Rank correlation between realised gain and the regulatory ΔNII estimate −0.13 (n=76) — the regulatory estimate does not describe the realised experience.
