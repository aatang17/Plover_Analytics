# AMD under Lisa Su — findings

All $ figures in millions unless stated. Fiscal years end late December (AMD, Intel); NVIDIA's
year ending late January is labelled by the calendar year it mostly covers. Calculated figures
are in data/derived.csv with their formulas; source rows are in log.md.

## Starting point, 2011–2015
- **Finding:** When Su was appointed (8 Oct 2014; 8-K, log #52), revenue had fallen for three years and the company was loss-making; in her first full year revenue fell a further 27.5% and shareholders' equity turned negative.
- **Numbers:** revenue 6,568 (2011) → 5,506 (2014), −16.2% (log #1; derived). 2015 revenue 3,991, −27.5%. Net income −1,183 (2012), −83 (2013), −403 (2014), −660 (2015) (log #6). Stockholders' equity 187 (2014) → −350 (2015) (log #23). Debt 2,212 (2014), 2,237 (2015) (log #16, #20–21). Cash + short-term investments 1,040 (2014), 785 (2015) (log #13–15). Free cash flow (CFO − capex) −193 (2014), −322 (2015) (log #7–8).
- **Segments:** Computing and Graphics 3,132 → 1,805 (−42.4%) in 2015; Enterprise, Embedded and Semi-Custom 2,374 → 2,186 (−7.9%) (log #38–40). In 2015 and 2016 the EESC segment was the larger of the two.

## Balance-sheet repair, 2016 and 2019 (H1)
- **Finding:** Equity was rebuilt in 2016, before growth resumed; debt was retired in 2019–2021, after it had. Both steps were funded largely with shares rather than cash from operations.
- **2016 (FY2016 10-K, log #53):** 115m new shares, proceeds 667; new long-term debt 782; repayment of 467 of 7.75% notes due 2020; warrant for 75m shares at $5.98 to West Coast Hitech L.P. (a Mubadala affiliate) under the GLOBALFOUNDRIES agreement amendment of 30 Aug 2016, recorded at 240; a limited-waiver payment of 100 to GLOBALFOUNDRIES (paid in full by end-2017, FY2017 10-K); gain of 146 on deconsolidation of the ATMP joint venture. Purchase obligation to GLOBALFOUNDRIES 3,300 at end-2016.
- **Result:** equity −350 → 477; debt 2,237 → 1,435; cash + STI 785 → 1,264 (2016).
- **2019 (FY2019 10-K, log #53):** warrant exercised 13 Feb 2019 for 75m shares, proceeds 449; 69m shares issued on conversion of 2.125% convertible notes due 2026; long-term debt repaid 403. Debt 1,250 (2018) → 486 (2019) → 330 (2020) → 1 (2021).
- **Revenue growth had begun by then:** +8.2% (2016), +21.6% (2017).
- **Hypothesis:** H1 partly supported. The equity repair preceded growth; the debt reduction followed it.

## R&D through the squeeze (H2)
- **Finding:** R&D was cut, but by less than revenue; from 2016 it rose every year.
- **Numbers:** R&D 1,354 (2012) → 947 (2015), −30.1%; 2014→2015 −11.7% against revenue −27.5% (log #4). R&D/revenue 19.5% (2014) → 23.7% (2015). R&D rose each year 2016–2025, to 8,091 in 2025 (8.5× 2015).
- **Hypothesis:** H2 partly supported: protected relative to revenue, not in dollars. Most of the cut predates Su (1,354 → 1,072 over 2012–2014).

## Growth with margin, 2016–2021 (H3)
- **Finding:** Revenue quadrupled from the 2015 trough to 2021 and gross margin rose 25.0pp, which indicates a change in mix and pricing, not volume alone.
- **Numbers:** revenue 3,991 (2015) → 16,434 (2021), 4.1×, 26.6% a year. Gross margin 23.2% (2016) → 34.0% (2017) → 42.6% (2019) → 48.2% (2021). Operating margin −8.6% (2016) → 22.2% (2021). First annual operating profit since 2013 in 2017 (127); first net profit since 2011 in 2018 (337). FCF 3,220 in 2021.
- **Segments:** Computing and Graphics 1,805 (2015) → 9,332 (2021), 5.2×; EESC 2,022 (2019) → 7,102 (2021).
- **Hypothesis:** H3 supported.

## Xilinx, 2022 (H4)
- **Finding:** Xilinx was paid for almost entirely in shares; the debt AMD carried afterwards was largely Xilinx's own.
- **Numbers (FY2022 10-K, log #53):** consideration 48.8bn, of which equity 48.5bn: 429m shares valued at $113.18. Goodwill 22,784 and intangibles 27,308 recognised; long-term debt assumed 1,474. AMD shares outstanding 1,207m → 1,612m (+405m); goodwill 289 → 24,177 (log #22, #24). Debt 1 → 2,467.
- **Cost to reported profit:** intangible amortisation 2,800 (2023), 2,400 (2024), 2,300 (2025) (log #50); 2023 amortisation was 7.0× operating income of 401. Operating margin 22.2% (2021) → 5.4% (2022) → 1.8% (2023) → 10.7% (2025), while gross margin stayed at 44.9–49.5%.
- **Also:** Pensando 1.7bn (May 2022), ZT Systems 4.4bn (31 Mar 2025), Silo AI 553 (2025).
- **Hypothesis:** H4 supported.

## Data Center mix, 2020–2025
- **Finding:** Data Center became AMD's largest segment, at about half of revenue since 2024.
- **Numbers:** Data Center 1,685 (2020) → 16,635 (2025), 9.9×; share of revenue 17.3% → 22.5% → 25.6% → 28.6% → 48.8% (2024) → 48.0% (2025). Gaming 6,805 (2022) → 2,595 (2024) → 3,910 (2025). FY2025 segments: Data Center 16,635; Client and Gaming 14,550 (Client 10,640, Gaming 3,910); Embedded 3,454.

## Position against Intel and NVIDIA
- **Finding:** AMD went from a tenth of Intel's revenue to two-thirds and now earns a higher gross margin than Intel; NVIDIA, which was smaller than AMD in 2014, is now more than six times its size.
- **Numbers (2014 → 2025):** AMD/Intel revenue 9.9% → 65.5%. AMD/NVIDIA 117.6% → 16.0%. Gross margin 2025: AMD 49.5%, Intel 34.8%, NVIDIA 71.1%. Operating margin 2025: AMD 10.7%, Intel −4.2%, NVIDIA 60.4%. R&D 2025: AMD 8,091, Intel 13,774, NVIDIA 18,497. Intel net loss 18,756 in 2024.

## CEO pay and shareholder return, 2020–2025
- **Finding:** AMD's cumulative TSR led the S&P 500 semiconductor index through 2023 and fell well behind it in 2024–2025; Su's reported pay was stable at about $30m until 2025.
- **Numbers (log #51):** summary comp 27.1m (2020), 29.5m, 30.2m, 30.3m, 31.0m (2024), 55.2m (2025). Compensation actually paid 178.4m, 188.4m, −160.1m, 189.8m, −18.7m, 146.7m. TSR (value of $100) AMD vs peer index: 199 vs 140 (2020), 317 vs 214, 140 vs 134, 319 vs 282, 271 vs 537 (2024), 234 vs 547 (2025).
- **Caveat (log #54):** the 2026 proxy footnote dates the $100 base to 24 Dec 2020, but its FY2021 value (317) is identical to the one in earlier proxies whose tables start at FY2020; the base is therefore uncertain. Only AMD-vs-index comparisons, which share a base, are used.
