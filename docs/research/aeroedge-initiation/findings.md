# AeroEdge (7409) — findings

## Product, customer and contract
- **Finding:** AeroEdge machines titanium-aluminide low-pressure-turbine blades for the CFM LEAP engine, is one of two qualified suppliers globally, and sells almost all of its output to Safran under a contract that runs to 2034.
- **Numbers:** Safran 96.8% of sales FY6/25 (annual report, log #11); 97.1% FY6/26 (deck, log #9). Share 35% → 40% from Jul 2024 → high-40s from Jan 2028 (log #9). Contract 2016–2034. No minimum volume; firm orders cover a few weeks; renewal not automatic; five termination/share-cut triggers (log #11).
- **Hypothesis:** context for H1.

## Volume driver
- **Finding:** Blade volume has tracked 39–43% of LEAP deliveries in each July–June year, consistent with the contracted share; FY6/26 volume rose 32.6% against LEAP deliveries +42.9%.
- **Numbers:** engine sets 563 / 573 / 639 / 847, LEAP 1,456 / 1,449 / 1,472 / 2,103 (log #8, #13); FY6/27E 1,084 (+28.0%) (log #9). Safran H1 2026 LEAP 1,030 (+41%), FY2026 "up high-teens" (log #10). Backlog 7,469 A320neo family + 5,404 737 MAX = 12.3 years each (log #9).
- **Hypothesis:** **supports H1.** Annual divergence ≤ 10 pp (FY6/25 +11.5% vs +1.6%, explained by the Jul 2024 share step-up; FY6/26 +32.6% vs +42.9%). Ratio calculated by us; shipments precede engine deliveries.

## Financial record
- **Finding:** Sales recovered from ¥848m (FY6/21) to ¥5,084m (FY6/26); operating margin 16.4% → 21.1% → 18.2% → 22.5%, guided 19.5% for FY6/27.
- **Numbers:** log #3, #4, #2. FY6/25 dip: SG&A +27.8% (806.8 → 1,031.4) while gross margin rose 45.1% → 46.8% (log #4). FY6/26 includes ~¥300m one-off, high-margin contract-development sales (log #8). FY6/27 margin decline attributed by the company to new-material development, D&A (¥417m → ¥767m) and hiring (log #9).
- **Hypothesis:** **breaks H2 as stated.** Contribution margin is high, but the company is reinvesting it: management targets ~20% OM while investing (log #9), so reported margin has not risen with volume.

## Unit economics and FX
- **Finding:** Revenue is a processing fee on Safran-supplied material, priced in USD, with an FX band clause since Oct 2024.
- **Numbers:** ¥40–50m OP per ¥1/USD; assumption ¥152 (log #9); FY6/26 average rate ¥152, FY6/25 ¥148, FY6/24 ¥145 (log #8).

## Growth pillars
- **Finding:** FY6/27 guidance shifts the mix: non-blade sales reach 29.9% of the total (Engine B ¥796m, Ono Plant ¥1,192m, other), against 5.5–9.1% in FY6/24–26.
- **Numbers:** sales_by_source.csv (log #9, #13). Own material: ~¥2.6bn capex, small volumes from FY6/27, profit contribution from FY6/29 (log #8). Engine A delayed ~1 year, contribution FY6/28 (log #8). MRO: R&D only (log #9).
- **Hypothesis:** **breaks H3.**

## Balance sheet and funding
- **Finding:** Capex is funded by operating cash, subsidies and bank debt; subsidies were the largest single source in FY6/26.
- **Numbers:** OCF ¥2,959m incl. subsidies ¥1,979m; ex-subsidy ¥980m on our basis (company states ¥1.01bn); fixed-asset spend ¥2,508m; Ono Plant −¥709m; LT loans +¥1,900m; cash ¥2,628m; net D/E 0.6x vs 1.5x ceiling; ¥3.8bn new loan agreements (log #8, #9). Borrowings ¥3,350m = 40.8% of total assets at FY6/25 (log #11).
- Share count +7.5% in FY6/26 (23,060,274 → 24,780,972, split-adjusted), in steps (log #7); capital stock ¥495.6m → ¥568m (log #3, #9); stock options = 578,000 potential shares, 15.0% of issued (FY6/25) (log #11); diluted EPS ¥29.63 vs basic ¥32.77 (log #2).
- **Hypothesis:** **supports H4** — no placement since the 2023 IPO; dilution comes from options. Confirm with the FY6/26 annual report.

## Ownership and governance
- **Finding:** Strategic and founding holders own a majority; foreign ownership is minimal; one disclosed short.
- **Numbers:** top five (Jun 2026) 56.1% (log #9, #13); foreign 2.6% (FY6/25 register, log #6); GS International short 0.6% (log #6); no dividend paid or planned (log #2, #11); board 7, one woman (log #6).

## Risks
- Contract terms (log #11); free-issued material from limited sources, past delays (log #11); first-time casting of own material (log #11); single plant in Tochigi (log #11); FX (log #9); Engine A delay (log #8); options overhang 15.0% (log #11); Safran FY2026 LEAP guide raised (log #10) — supply chain constraint on A320neo from the competing PW1100G engine noted by the company (log #8).
